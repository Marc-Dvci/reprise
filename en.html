REPRISE is a lunar workshop for making, repairing and reusing solar reflectors. Robots coat and renew film in vacuum; people diagnose machines, repair removable parts and approve their return to service. Three production courts meet a covered transfer street beside a regolith-shielded workplace. A shared table, repair benches and quiet rooms make maintenance a collective way of inhabiting the Moon. An Earth demonstrator begins the path towards a permanent human presence supported by a lunar settlement.
